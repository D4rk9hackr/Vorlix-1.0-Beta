It is not a live comparison against any specific product.

## Caveats

1. **Vorlix tokens shown are estimates** — minimal LLM overhead for tool choice (~100 tokens per action). A real agent may spend more on planning.

2. **CV time estimates are conservative** — actual vision-loop latency can be higher due to network latency, cold starts, and rate limits.

3. **Single-run measurements** — wall-clock times vary with system load.
